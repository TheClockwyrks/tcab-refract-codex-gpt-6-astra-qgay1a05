# Refract

Light finds a way. Give it a path. Refract is a quiet puzzle of luminous beams, geometric lenses, and shared possibilities on a dark optical bench.

Press an outlined emitter and draw through its matching lenses to reach the other emitter. Follow neighboring nodes, turn diagonally, and weave around the other channels. Crystals welcome any color, but every charge must be spent exactly. A board is complete when every channel is connected and every crystal is satisfied.

Change your mind as often as you like. Release to keep a path, pick up an end to continue, or trace backward to unwind. There is no clock to race and no move limit—just the pleasure of finding a picture where every piece belongs.

Follow the Campaign through 24 hand-built studies, from a single thread of light to a full spectrum of intertwined paths. Or enter Cascade, an endless sequence of fresh puzzles that grows more intricate every five solves. Three shapes, three colors, and many ways to see the answer.
